THE MISSING LAYER

The hardest part is between reading and writing.

Discovery tools help us find papers. Reading tools help us understand individual papers. Writing tools help us draft. Evidentia focuses on the fragmented middle: how claims, evidence, conditions, and research decisions connect across a body of literature.

TRUST BY DESIGN

Scholarly judgment stays with the researcher.

Future AI features remain assistive. They may suggest a theme, relationship, or possible tension, but the researcher must confirm, modify, or reject the suggestion.

Evidence First

Important claims point back to source evidence.

Human Judgment

AI suggestions never silently become research conclusions.

Context Travels

Evidence snippets retain source location and relevant context.

No False Authority

No invented quality score, credibility score, or automatic declaration of a research gap.

SCOPE BOUNDARY

Public, non-sensitive research only.

Evidentia intentionally avoids confidential, restricted, proprietary, operationally sensitive, or high-risk personal material.

Your projects stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
